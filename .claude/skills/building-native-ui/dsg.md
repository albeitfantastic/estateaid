## Brand

| Brand / tint | `#234536` |

Tone: calm, warm, premium. No urgency colours. No orange, red, or countdown-style treatments in marketing surfaces.

## App tokens

**Forest + ink + warm stone** — see `constants/theme.ts`. Warm near-black body text (`#252220`), stone borders (`#DED9D0`), parchment surfaces (`#F6F4EF` / `#FFFCF9`). `BrandTint` for primary actions.

Use `tintMuted` for soft fills behind icons and chips; keep primary actions and selected states on `tint` / `BrandTint` in light mode.
